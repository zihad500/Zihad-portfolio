About Me

I'm an EEE student with interests in leadership, event organisation, military history, and sport. My interest in military service developed through my school BNCC experience.

As a BNCC Cadet Sergeant, I attended a camp at the Bangladesh Military Academy and later led the Government Muslim High School contingent in the Victory Day Parade 2020 in Chittagong.

My ISSB journey took me to the final stage twice, in January and August 2026. Although I was not selected, those experiences taught me the importance of hard work and teamwork. They remain an important part of my story, but they do not define all that I can become.
